History of Porsche Design

Ferdinand Porsche

Founded in 1931 in Stuttgart by Ferdinand Porsche. The brand pioneered lightweight automotive architecture and rear-engine innovation.

Ferdinand Alexander "Butzi" Porsche

Grandson of the founder, he designed the iconic Porsche 911 in 1963, creating the world-renowned sloping flyline silhouettes.

Modern E-Performance

Pioneered 800-volt high-performance electric architecture with the Taycan while preserving race-bred dynamics across all models.
