Brand History

1931 — The Founding

Founded by Ferdinand Porsche in Stuttgart, Germany. The company initially provided vehicle development and engineering consultation before producing its own sports cars.

1948 — Porsche 356

Ferry Porsche, son of Ferdinand, created the first vehicle to bear the Porsche nameplate—the 356. It laid the foundation for lightweight, precise sports cars.

1963 — The 911 Icon

Unveiled at the Frankfurt Motor Show, the legendary rear-engine 911 was introduced, cementing Porsche's signature identity and racing pedigree.

Design Pioneers

Ferdinand Porsche

The engineering visionary who established the firm and pioneered aerodynamic, rear-engine vehicle concepts that shaped modern automotive design.

F. A. Porsche

Grandson of the founder, Ferdinand Alexander "Butzi" Porsche styled the timeless silhouette of the original 911, establishing Porsche's design language.

Michael Mauer

Porsche’s Chief Designer (since 2004), responsible for evolving classic proportions into modern electric and hybrid eras with models like the Taycan.
